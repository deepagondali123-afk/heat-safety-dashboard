Frequently Asked Questions

1 What is HeatShield?
HeatShield transforms workplace heat data into zone-level risk intelligence, helps prioritize limited safety resources, recommends protective actions, and records what actually happened.
2 What does the Priority Score represent?
It represents the relative urgency of a workplace zone based on factors such as heat severity, worker exposure, and exposure duration.
3 Why is Zone A prioritized?
Zone A combines critical heat conditions, high worker exposure, and prolonged exposure, resulting in the highest priority in the prototype scenario.
4 What happens when a resource is deployed?
The deployment can be recorded against the selected zone, allowing the system to track what resource was used and when the action occurred.
5 How does Incident History help?
It creates a timestamped record of recommendations, actions, deployments, and outcomes so safety actions can be reviewed later.
6 Is the AI output a live machine-learning prediction?
The current prototype demonstrates the prioritization workflow using example data. The displayed AI indicators should not be interpreted as live machine-learning outputs.
